D-Mart Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard
 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard using Looker Studio to analyze D-Mart's sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, payment preferences, and regional trends. The dashboard converts raw retail data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and support data-driven decision-making.

 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, making it difficult to identify performance trends and growth opportunities. This dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance, helping management monitor KPIs, evaluate customer behavior, and identify areas for strategic improvement.

 Business Requirements (BRD)

The dashboard was designed to:

Monitor key business KPIs.
Compare sales and profit across regions and product categories.
Analyze customer segments and payment methods.
Identify high-performing and underperforming business areas.
Support strategic business decisions through interactive reporting.

  Key Business Insights
Achieved ₹1.3M in total sales with ₹236K in profit.
West region generated the highest sales and profitability, indicating a strong market presence.
South region recorded the lowest performance, highlighting opportunities for targeted business expansion.
Clothing emerged as the highest revenue-generating product category.
Regular customers contributed the largest share of revenue, emphasizing the importance of customer retention.
Cash remained the preferred payment method, suggesting opportunities to encourage digital payment adoption.

 Business Analysis
 
SWOT Analysis

Strengths

Strong overall profitability
High-performing West region
Loyal customer base

Weaknesses

Underperforming South region
High dependence on a single product category

Opportunities

Expand marketing in low-performing regions
Increase digital payment adoption
Strengthen customer loyalty programs

Threats

Competitive retail market
Margin pressure due to discounting
Changing consumer purchasing behavior

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Issue: South region recorded the lowest sales and profit.

Possible Causes

Lower customer demand
Limited promotional activities
Product assortment gaps
Regional competition

Recommendations

Implement region-specific marketing campaigns.
Optimize inventory based on local demand.
Benchmark successful strategies from the West region.
